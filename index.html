Korea Blockchain Week 2026 - selected events

Seoul, 29 Sep - 1 Oct 2026 · Alexey Mokropulo, CPO, My Neobank · all times Seoul time (KST)
These are the main KBW 2026 events selected by Alexey. All events below are confirmed by the hosts. A number of other applications are still waiting for approval (listed at the end).

Tue 29 Sep

18:30-23:00Stabull Dinner
Co-hosts: Aracore, Kbank, HashKey, Anchorage Digital
Private dinner for senior people from Korean financial institutions and global stablecoin companies. No panels, just dinner and conversation.
Gangnam
19:00-22:00Seoul & Settlement: Cross-border Payments Night
Hosts: Native, BDACS
Private salon for about 40 senior leaders in stablecoin payments: how to make cross-border settlement faster, cheaper and more compliant. Centred on KRW1, the Korean won stablecoin by BDACS, and the payment rails around it.
Seoul
19:00-01:00THE LOCAL: Pints-as-a-Service
Host: LO:TECH (London digital asset trading firm)
Informal evening for founders, investors and builders. No pitching, relaxed networking.
Seoul

Wed 30 Sep

16:00-21:00Proof of Liquidity: Seoul Edition
Host: Yield Network · co-hosts Monad, Shift DeFi, Frankencoin, Apyx · partners Bitmaker, Ember, Zama
Capital formation event without panels: a curated floor where capital (funds, market makers, family offices, treasuries) meets projects looking for liquidity (L2s, RWA issuers, protocols).
Seoul
17:30-22:00Plasma x Seoul: Spend It Like Money
Co-hosts: Tether, Plasma, Rain, Stripe
Stablecoin payments evening aimed at builders of stablecoin infrastructure, payments and neobanks. Panel "From mint to checkout" with Plasma (CSO), Rain (CEO), Tether (APAC) and Stripe (Head of Crypto GTM), plus a Plasma One product demo.
Cheongdam, Gangnam
18:00-22:00BitGo x OP Labs x Plan G Networking Drinks
Hosts: BitGo, Optimism, Plan G Research
Networking drinks hosted by BitGo (custody, wallets, stablecoins and settlement; Official Institutional Sponsor of KBW), Optimism and Plan G Research.
Seoul
18:00-21:00KBW Investor & Trader Reception
Hosts: QUOTE.TRADE, KTX
Curated evening for founders, investors, traders and industry leaders, with food and drinks.
Seongsu

Thu 1 Oct

13:00-18:00Bridge Seoul 2026
Host: Wavebridge · about 250 invited guests
Institutional conference on Korea's on-chain finance: spot ETFs, the Digital Asset Basic Act and won stablecoins. Includes a session on cross-border payments and stablecoin rails with KB Securities, Canton Foundation, BC Card, Kbank and KakaoBank.
Yeouido
14:30-18:30Bridging Finance Onchain
Hosts: KYOBO Life, Circle, SBI Holdings, BDACS
How traditional finance moves on-chain: stablecoins, on-chain FX and tokenised assets. Talks by Kyobo, Circle and Startale Group (SBI), a live demo of KRW1 to USDC settlement by BDACS, then a panel and networking.
Four Seasons Hotel Seoul
17:30-21:00TCH Venture Connect
Host: The Crypto Hub
Curated evening connecting founders, investors and ecosystem leaders, with short introductions to new projects.
Seoul
19:30-23:00KBW VIP Mixer
Hosts: Stellar Development Foundation, Exodus, OSL Group
Invite-only evening for senior people in crypto and fintech: founders, funds, exchanges, market makers and infrastructure teams.
Apgujeong, Gangnam
